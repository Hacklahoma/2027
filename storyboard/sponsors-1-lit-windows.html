Lit Windows Skyline

The sponsor section is a night riverside skyline whose lit windows are the sponsor panes, mirrored in the river below.

  1. Tier is the size and warmth of light.Title is a huge arched window with a flickering glow, Gold are medium windows, Silver and Community are small panes with a paler light.
  2. Windows switch on as you arrive.A staggered cascade lights the skyline left to right as it scrolls into view, and the Title window comes on last. Reduced motion shows it already lit.
  3. Hover or focus brightens and reveals.The pane goes cream-bright and a tag shows the sponsor name with a Visit button. The river reflection ripples and mirrors every state.
