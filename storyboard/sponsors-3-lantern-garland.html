Lantern Garland

Sponsors are paper lanterns strung on sagging wires across the night sky. Size and glow tell the tier, the logo is printed on the lantern face, and one last lantern waits unlit for you.

  1. Size and glow are the tier

    One big haloed Title lantern, then Gold, Silver and tiny Community lanterns. Hierarchy reads at a glance, and the tier is always written out too, never colour alone.

  2. Hover, focus or tap to meet them

    The lantern swings and brightens, and a caption card shows tier plus a Visit link. Keyboard focus does the same; on touch, the first tap opens the card.

  3. The unlit lantern is the CTA

    Last on the wire, dark until you reach for it, then it ignites with a burst of sparks. "Light a lantern with us" says the call to action without a button.

  • Stacked wires, one tier per wire

    Title on its own wire, Gold pair below, then Silver trio, then Community lanterns. Wires slope in alternating directions so the garland zig-zags down the page and runs off both screen edges, as if it continues.

  • Touch-friendly by construction

    Every lantern is at least 52 by 81px (the whole lantern is the hit area). The first tap swings it and opens the card, the 44px "Visit website" button inside the card navigates. Tapping elsewhere closes it.

  • The CTA closes the garland

    The unlit lantern sits at the end of the last wire, right-aligned so the thumb reaches it. Tap to ignite, tap again to open the email.

  • Calm by default, accessible always

    Sway, embers and flicker stop under prefers-reduced-motion; lanterns stay lit and static. Each lantern is a real link with a spoken name and tier, the wires and embers are hidden from screen readers, and tab order runs Title, Gold, Silver, Community, then the CTA.

C States & interactions live panels: they animate and respond just like the real thing

1

Default: swaying

Each lantern sways on its own rhythm; small lanterns swing wider, the big one barely moves. Embers drift up, fairy lights twinkle. Reduced motion: everything holds still.

2

Hover or focus

The lantern swings, its glow swells and the caption card opens with tier and a Visit link. Keyboard focus triggers the same state, with a visible ring.

3

Tap on touch

First tap opens the card (the ring shows the tap). The 44px Visit button inside the card does the navigating. Tap outside, or press Esc, to put it away.

4

CTA ignites

The call to action starts unlit with a faint ember at its wick. Hover, focus or tap and it floods with light and throws sparks. Try the left one.
