The Editorial Ledger

A program’s patron page, printed on cream paper and held up to the night: calm, ruled, and built so every logo reads clearly.

  1. Quiet until you lookEvery logo sits in the same navy ink. Hover or focus and it blooms to full colour, so no brand shouts over another.
  2. Rank by the ruled ledgerTier numerals, small-caps labels and hairline rules do the ranking. Logo slots step down 1 → 2 → 3 → list.
  3. Paper is trustOne bright cream panel against the night page. On mobile each tier collapses to full-width rows, one logo per row, 88px tall tap targets.
