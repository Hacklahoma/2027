What changes on a phone

  • Three things only. Logo, a compact Register pill, and a 44px kebab. Links and past years move into the sheet.
  • Light-from-the-kebab reveal. The sheet opens as a circle of lamplight growing from the button, then chapters rise in one by one.
  • Chapters, not a list. Large Gloock numerals I to IV; a lit dot marks where you are now.
  • Existing menu content kept. Exec team, Register, Lights we have lit before (2026 to 2018), Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord, MLH Code of Conduct.

Statesand interactions each frame is the real component, held in one state

Accessibility

Real nav, lists and buttons. aria-current on the active chapter, aria-expanded on the menu button, visible gold focus rings, every target at least 44px.

Reduced motion

The sheet fades in without the circle reveal or stagger, and the nav still shows state through color and the progress line.

Logo

The navy wordmark is recolored cream with an SVG color-matrix filter (gold on hover), so no second asset is needed.
