The Chapter Rail

No bar at all. A thin riverbank down the left edge, where the page’s chapters are stops on a line of light and a small boat marks how far you’ve drifted.

  1. I

    Chapters, not links

    Numerals I–V sit tiny on the bank. Reach for one (hover, focus, or tap) and its name slides out in italic serif. Nothing else competes with the page.

  2. II

    Progress you can see

    The line fills with lantern light as you scroll, a boat rides it, and the current chapter’s numeral glows like a lit lantern.

  3. III

    One corner, one kebab

    Past years, exec team and socials stay in the existing full-screen menu. On phones the rail lies down into a bottom scrubber you can tap or drag.

On a phone

  • Slim bottom bar (74px) holds the line, the boat and six numerals: a top stop plus I–V.
  • Tap a numeral to jump; each stop is a 56×68px target.
  • Drag anywhere along the bar to scrub. The boat follows your thumb and a bubble names the chapter.
  • Kebab sits top-right, wordmark top-left. Nothing else floats on the page.

Built in

  • Real <nav>, <ol> and <button>s; the current chapter gets aria-current.
  • Labels are reachable by keyboard focus, not just hover, with a gold focus ring.
  • Reduced motion: the boat and fill still track scroll, but flicker and slide-out easing are off.
