Floating Dock

A glass pill hung over the river like a lantern. It lights the chapter you are reading, then gets out of your way.

  1. i.Hung, not stuck

    A detached glass pill with a gold hairline and soft glow floats off the top edge, so the night sky stays visible around it. On phones it drops to a thumb-reach dock.

  2. ii.Spotlight, not underline

    A sliding gold highlight follows scroll-spy from link to link. The lantern mark unfolds into the full wordmark on hover or focus. A live countdown keeps the date in view.

  3. iii.Hides, never vanishes

    It lifts away on scroll down and returns on scroll up. While hidden, a tiny swinging lantern button stays so the way back is always one tap away.

How the dock adapts

  • Bottom, thumb reach. The pill moves to the bottom edge, 12px in from the sides, floating with the same glass and gold hairline.
  • Four items plus Register. Icon and label per chapter, each a 54px target. Register rises out of the center as a gold lantern disc.
  • Countdown becomes a tag. The date chip shrinks to a cream "86d" tag pinned to the Register disc.
  • Safe-area aware. Sits above the home indicator using env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • Same hide rule. Scroll down and the dock slides away, leaving a small lantern at the bottom center. Scroll up or tap it to return.
III.

States & interactions

Four beats of the same dock, desktop width, scaled down.

Prefers-reduced-motion: the flicker, swing, ping and slides are switched off and the spotlight jumps instead of gliding. The register link and wordmark are real links; the dock is a <nav aria-label="Primary"> with a list of links.
