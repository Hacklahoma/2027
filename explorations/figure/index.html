03 · How it's put together

A painting in a live world

Four small pieces make the painting belong to the scene.

A standing card

The painting stands at the waterline and turns to face the camera as it drifts, so it never looks flat.

A real reflection

The water reflects it like everything else: darker, and broken up by the ripples.

A real light

An invisible boat sits exactly where the painted one is, so their candle lights the water around them and flickers with the fleet.

One rule: eye level

The painting was made from eye level, so the camera has to stay near eye level too. From high above, any painted figure reads as a flat cut-out. The hero layout should be framed around this.

Breathing light

The warm parts of the painting (face, hands, the boat) brighten and dim with that same flicker. The rest of the figure stays still.
